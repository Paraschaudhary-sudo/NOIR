NOIR NOTES / 03

01 / BUILD YOUR BASE

Start with pieces that work harder.

A good wardrobe begins with versatile essentials. Let fit, texture and comfort lead; add statement pieces when you want the mood to shift.

02 / FIND YOUR PALETTE

Colour is a feeling.

Choose shades you naturally reach for, then bring in one unexpected tone. Personal style grows when you experiment without rules.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

Wear the outfit. Own the moment.

Roll a sleeve, layer a shirt, switch your shoes. The small choices are often what make an outfit feel unmistakably yours.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE IDEA

A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

Hey, I'm Paras.

NOIR began with a simple thought: style should feel personal, not prescribed.

I’m Paras Chaudhary, the person behind NOIR. I wanted to build a space for pieces that feel confident without trying too hard—clothes you can make your own, in your own way.

This is a growing creative project, built around curiosity, self-expression and the belief that your style should tell your story.

With intention, Paras

SO, WHAT IS NOIR?

02 / OUR POINT OF VIEW

A wardrobe is more than clothes. It's a point of view.

NOIR is an independent fashion concept built on modern silhouettes, versatile pieces and individual expression. We bring western, traditional and everyday styles into one place—so your wardrobe can move with every version of you.

No pressure to follow every trend. No one way to dress. Just a little more intention, a little more confidence, and room to make it yours.
