A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER · 001

The house of NOIR

Meet the vision.
Meet the founder.

Paras Chaudhary — mostly known as Paras

NOIR begins with a simple ambition: to build a luxury clothing brand that feels personal, expressive, and thoughtfully made. Paras’s vision is to build NOIR into a distinctive luxury clothing brand with its own identity — where confident silhouettes, elevated everyday essentials, and thoughtful details come together. The goal is to make fashion feel personal and expressive: pieces that speak quietly, feel considered, and help people show up as themselves.

Beyond the brand, Paras is a chill, nonchalant, and open-minded guy who values fresh perspectives and the freedom to explore new ideas. He believes good style is personal, creativity has no single formula, and growth comes from staying curious. NOIR is his starting point — a long-term vision he wants to shape one thoughtful step at a time.

With intention, Paras.
